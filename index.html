For Cara · on her birthday

Happy 18th birthday Cara! You're 18. That's mad. You're legally an adult now, which is a weird thing to say about a monkey chud.

You can drink legally now. Not that that was ever stopping you, lmao. You can vote too if you can be bothered. And apparently the responsibilities are coming? As if you didn't already have them. You've been acting like you're 40 for years.

I hope you have a good day. I hope the rest of the celebrations are just as good, and I hope you're with the people who make you happy. Let them spoil you a bit, you've earned it.

Half a world away, still one of my favourite people.

I'm really glad I met you. We're a long way apart and on completely different clocks, but out of everyone I talk to, you're one of my favourites. I don't take that for granted.

The gift is small. I know. But I wanted to do something more than just send a happy birthday message, even if this is a bit much. I hope it makes today a bit better.

I'm free basically all day if you want to call. Just message me first. Seriously, ring me, text me, send me a photo of your cake and I'll answer.

P.S. You're an adult now, so technically you're allowed to be the responsible one. Don't rush into it.

always, your friend
